# Asset provenance

This ledger is the publication authority for bundled source and visual material.
Every shipped row is cleared for redistribution; anything unresolved remains
outside the repository and its public history.

| Material | Shipped path | Source | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SynthCity TypeScript, HTML, CSS, tests, and procedural visuals | `src/`, `design-review/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, selected `public/design-review/*.js` | Authored for SynthCity | Cleared | Preserve the root MIT license. |
| Three.js runtime and loader modules | `public/vendor/three/` | Three.js project | Cleared | Preserve `public/vendor/three/LICENSE`. |
| Scenario reports | `evidence/market-city-scenarios/`, `evidence/market-city-fire-scenarios/` | Generated by deterministic SynthCity tests | Cleared | Keep reports bounded and free of local paths or private city data. |
| Emoji and system fonts | Runtime/browser rendering | Player platform | Cleared | Do not copy platform artwork or font binaries into the repository. |

## Intake requirements

Every new bundled asset must record:

- Original source URL or project-owned source file.
- Author or rights holder.
- License and required attribution.
- Confirmation that embedded textures, logos, trademarks, and likenesses are
  also cleared.
- Conversion or generation steps.
- Reviewer and approval date.

Free-to-download is not a redistribution license. Generated output is not
automatically cleared when its inputs or source material are unknown.

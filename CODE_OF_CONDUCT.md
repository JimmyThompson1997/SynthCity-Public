# Community standards

SynthCity discussions should be useful, specific, and respectful.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, clear evidence,
patience with contributors of different experience levels, and respect for
privacy and intellectual-property boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexualized conduct, spam,
and knowingly submitting stolen or unauthorized material are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may
restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub rather
than escalating them in a public thread.
